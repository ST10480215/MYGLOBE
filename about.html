ABOUT SOLSTICE SOLAR SOLUTIONS

Solstice Solar Solutions was founded in 2019 by Mnqweno Mankayi, an electrical engineer who saw the need for reliable solar power during South Africa's load-shedding crisis. He started the business from his garage in Westering, Gqeberha, installing small systems for neighbours and local shops.

By 2021, the company grew into a fully accredited installation business with a showroom in Newton Park, Gqeberha. Today, Solstice Solar Solutions has completed over 500 installations and operates across the Eastern Cape and Western Cape, helping clients save money and reduce their reliance on Eskom.


Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality solar energy solutions to homes and businesses across the Eastern and Western Cape through expert installation and reliable after-sales support.


Our Vision

To become the most trusted solar installation brand in the Eastern and Western Cape by 2030.
